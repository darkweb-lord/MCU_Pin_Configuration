Device Overview

PIC24FV32KA304 family features & specifications

PIC24FV32KA304 — 16-bit eXtreme Low Power MCU. 32 KB Flash, 2 KB RAM, 512 B data EEPROM, up to 32 MHz, 2.0–5.5 V operation. Peripherals: 2× UART, 2× SPI, 2× I²C, 16-channel 12-bit ADC, 5× 16-bit timers, 3× Input Capture, 3× Output Compare/PWM, 3× comparators, CTMU, RTCC and CRC. Reference: datasheet DS39995C.
